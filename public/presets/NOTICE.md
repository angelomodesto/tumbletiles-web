# Preset configurations

The `.xml` files in this directory are board configurations from the TumbleTiles
project maintained by the Algorithmic Self-Assembly Research Group at UTRGV,
taken from the `Verification/` directory of:

https://github.com/ButterM-40/TumbleTiles

They are included so the simulator can reproduce the constructions used in the
research. All credit for these configurations belongs to their authors.

Note: at the time they were copied that repository carried no licence file, so
redistribution terms are not stated. If the authors would prefer these were not
included here, they will be removed.

`x-monotonc repcator.xml` from the same directory is not included: its board is
250 by 250, beyond this simulator's 100 by 100 limit.
